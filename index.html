Executive Threat Summary

Origin: State-Sponsored (Storm-0940)

In October 2026, Microsoft Threat Intelligence disclosed a sophisticated campaign attributed to state-sponsored threat actor Storm-0940. The adversary compromised the supply chain of ConnectWise Cloud Backup SaaS infrastructure to harvest elevated OAuth application credentials and secrets.

Armed with stolen OAuth secrets, Storm-0940 deployed ARTEX, an advanced agentic AI framework capable of operating autonomous agent meshes. ARTEX used direct Microsoft Graph API calls to execute non-interactive sign-ins, effectively bypassing traditional Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), rapidly enumerating target environments, escalating privileges, and establishing persistent backdoors across thousands of Microsoft 365 enterprise tenants within hours.

Critical Operational Finding

Traditional conditional access and interactive MFA enforcement were bypassed because the stolen OAuth credentials allowed direct service principal authentication via Microsoft Graph API endpoints. High-volume password spraying (over 151,000 requests) was coordinated concurrently by ARTEX agents originating from infrastructure including 144.172.64.99 to discover additional initial access vectors.

Primary Targets

  • Defense & Aerospace Government defense contractors and military supply chain vendors.
  • Critical Energy Infrastructure Power grid operators, nuclear tech, and smart-grid SaaS vendors.
  • IT & Cloud Managed Services MSPs with delegated administrative privileges into client tenants.
  • Financial Institutions High-value enterprise banking & cloud payment clearing houses.
